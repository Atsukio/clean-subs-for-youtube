# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 10, 2026**

Clean Subs for YouTube changes the layout of YouTube's Subscriptions page. This policy describes the information the extension handles.

## Information handled

When you visit YouTube, the extension checks the current page URL so it can activate only on the Subscriptions page. On that page, it reads section headings and video or Shorts links to identify and organize the feed. It also saves your selected Videos or Shorts tab in the browser's `sessionStorage` so the selection can be restored during the browser tab's session.

## How information is used and shared

This information is processed locally in your browser to provide the extension's features. The extension does not send it to the developer or any other server, sell it, or share it with third parties. The extension does not use analytics or collect information for advertising.

The selected tab preference is stored in the page's `sessionStorage` and is available for that tab's browser session. The extension does not maintain a separate database of browsing history or page content.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the publisher through the contact information on the Clean Subs for YouTube Chrome Web Store listing.
